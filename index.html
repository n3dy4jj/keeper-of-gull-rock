The Keeper of Gull Rock

The Light Goes Out

It is 11pm on Gull Rock and the storm is hitting hard enough to rattle the glass in the window frames. You are the new lighthouse keeper, and this is only your third night alone on the island. Above you the great lamp flickers twice, buzzes like a trapped insect, then dies. The whole coast goes black. Somewhere out in that dark, boats are steering by a light that is no longer there.

  • Climb the tower to check the lamp
  • Run to the radio room for help
