The Keeper of Gull Rock

William's Diary

The diary is full of careful, tidy notes about the lamp, the weather and every ship that passed the island. The last page is different. It has a hand drawn map of the basement with an arrow pointing behind the stairs to a hidden fuse box that nobody has written about anywhere else. In the margin beside the arrow, in much heavier pencil, William has written one line: "If the light fails, she comes looking for it."
