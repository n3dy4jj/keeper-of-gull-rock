The Keeper of Gull Rock

Ending: Silence

You reach over and switch the radio off, and the room goes completely quiet. For the rest of the night you sit in the dark listening to the storm throw itself at the windows, telling yourself it was interference, or a hoax, or nothing at all. At sunrise you walk down to the rocks. There is broken timber scattered along the waterline, pale and splintered, and none of it is new. You hand in your notice the following week. You never take another keeper job, and you never explain to anyone why.

THE END
