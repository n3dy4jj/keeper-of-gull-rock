A Familiar Name

"Captain Hart," the voice replies. "And our keeper is William Hart, my brother. Is he there with you?" A cold feeling runs all the way down your back. The keeper's cottage next door still has W. HART carved into the door frame in neat capital letters. Nobody has lived in that cottage for seventy years, and the council keeps the key in a drawer in your office.
