Closer Than It Should Be

You hold the button down and ask, straight out, who you are talking to. The reply comes back slow and very clear, as if whoever is speaking has all the time in the world. "Mary Ellen, out of Sydney, four days at sea." You tell them there is no Mary Ellen anywhere on the register. There is a long pause with voices moving behind it. Then: "We sailed on the 4th of June, 1954." You realise the rain has stopped hitting the window, and you cannot remember when it stopped.
