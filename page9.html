The Keeper of Gull Rock

Dots and Dashes

You aim the torch out to sea and flash S.O.S. over and over until your arm aches and your eyes start to blur. For a long time nothing happens at all. Then, far out in the dark, much further out than the fishing boat, a light flashes back at you. It is slow, steady and patient, and it spells out three words: W-H-O A-R-E Y-O-U.

  • Flash back your name
  • Flash: TURN NORTH NOW
