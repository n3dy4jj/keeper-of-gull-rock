The Keeper of Gull Rock

The Logbook

The last proper entry is dated 4 June 1954 and the handwriting is shaky enough that you have to read it twice. "Lamp failed. Mary Ellen lost with all hands. My fault." Below that, in ink that somehow still looks wet, someone has added four more words: "Keep the light on." Pressed flat between the next two pages is a pencil sketch of a steamer with a lamp drawn on its mast and the word GREEN circled beside it.
