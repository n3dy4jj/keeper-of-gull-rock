The Keeper of Gull Rock

Ending: The Name in the Log

"Then it's up to you," the captain says, and the radio dies in the middle of his next sentence. You open the logbook to the very back page, where you have never looked before. There is a list of names running down it, one for every keeper since 1954, in fourteen different kinds of handwriting. Beside each name is the same short line: "I heard the Mary Ellen." The pen is still sitting in the gutter of the book where the last keeper put it down. You sign your name under the others, close the book, and go upstairs to fix the light.
