The Beach

You stumble down the wet path to the beach, where the waves are coming in hard enough to flatten the sand. An old wooden rowboat is still tied to the jetty, bumping against the posts with every swell. The fishing boat is only a few hundred metres out now, close enough that you can hear somebody on board shouting, though you cannot make out the words.
