The Keeper of Gull Rock

Ending: Morning Inspector

You decide the stairs and the water are too dangerous in the dark, so you wait the storm out with a blanket and a cold cup of tea. The inspector's boat ties up just after seven in the morning. He walks straight down to the basement, finds the spare fuses sitting on the shelf where they have always been, and has the lamp running again in about five minutes. Then he comes back up, looks at you for a while, and asks why you never went down to look. You stand there with absolutely no answer, and he writes something in his folder that you do not get to read.
