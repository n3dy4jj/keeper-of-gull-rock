Red Sky

The flare bursts open above the water and turns the falling rain bright red. For about three seconds you can see everything: the boat, the white water, the black teeth of the reef. The boat does turn. It turns the wrong way, straight towards the rocks, and you realise the crew cannot see the reef at all from where they are sitting.
